Entry for the make.fun competition "The Self-Checkout Is Judging You": one funny single-line remark in the voice of a judgmental self-checkout machine, 1 to 200 characters, smug and petty about the shopper's basket
`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the gate first, then reading
the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> Please place your twelve yoghurts in the bagging area, and your life choices in the returns queue.

Why this entry: This line wins the pairwise rounds because it speaks in the machine's own voice (Please place ... in the bagging area), it is smug and petty about the basket and the bagging, the sting lands cold with no context (your life choices go in the returns queue), it is tight with no setup padding, and it is fresh — it never repeats the tired unexpected-item-in-the-bagging-area joke. Backup: You scanned twelve yoghurts. I counted thirteen. One of them is judging you too.
